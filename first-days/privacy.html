First Days Privacy Policy Last updated: August 21, 2026 First Days is a baby diary intended for parents and other adult caregivers. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information required to operate and improve the app. For privacy questions, contact us at hi@wordspire.app. 1. Diary data First Days allows you to record information about a child and caregiving activities, including: - name or nickname and date of birth; - sleep and awake periods; - feeding and food; - diapers; - walks; - measurements; - medication, medical appointments and vaccinations; - notes and other information entered by the user. This information is stored locally on the user’s device. We do not receive the contents of the diary or use them for advertising. You decide what information to enter into the app. 2. Data export First Days can generate reports and export information in formats such as PDF, CSV or JSON. An exported file is sent only to the application or storage location selected by the user through the iOS share interface. Further processing is governed by the privacy policy of the selected service. 3. Location, motion and weather With the user’s permission, First Days may use: - location to record a walking route and retrieve weather information for the walk; - motion data to estimate steps during a walk. These permissions are used only for the relevant features. Recorded routes remain in the diary on the device. To retrieve weather information, coordinates and request time may be sent to Apple WeatherKit or another available weather provider. AppMetrica is not used to record walking routes, and precise location reporting to AppMetrica is disabled. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. 4. Analytics and diagnostics We use AppMetrica to understand feature usage, identify errors and improve app stability. AppMetrica may process: - device model and operating-system version; - app version; - language and region; - technical app or device identifiers; - IP address; - pseudonymous usage events; - crash and performance information; - push-notification delivery and interaction information. We do not include children’s names, birth dates, diary notes, medical information, medication names, diary contents, walking routes or exported files in analytics events. See the Yandex Privacy Policy and AppMetrica documentation. 5. Subscriptions and purchases First Days uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user identifier, device and platform information, product and subscription status, and Apple receipt information required to validate purchases. Payments are processed by Apple. We and RevenueCat do not receive your payment-card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. 6. Notifications First Days may send local reminders about sleep, feeding, medication, vaccinations, appointments and other events, as well as informational push notifications. Apple and AppMetrica may process a technical push token and notification delivery or interaction information. Notifications can be disabled in iOS Settings. 7. Support requests If you contact hi@wordspire.app, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. Please do not send diary contents, medical information, routes or other sensitive data unless it is necessary to resolve your request. 8. Sharing with third parties We do not sell personal information or use diary contents for personalized advertising. Limited technical data may be processed by Apple, RevenueCat, AppMetrica and a weather provider for the purposes described above. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. 9. Retention and deletion Diary information remains on the device until the user deletes the relevant records, child profile or app. Deleting the app may delete local data unless the user has created an export or backup. Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription. Third-party services retain technical data according to their own policies. 10. Children’s privacy First Days is intended for parents and adult caregivers, not for independent use by children. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. Information about a child is entered by an adult who is responsible for having the appropriate authority to process it. 11. Security We use available iOS security mechanisms to protect local app storage. However, no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their devices, backups and exported files. 12. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal-data processing. Most diary content is stored only on your device and can be deleted directly in the app. Contact hi@wordspire.app with privacy requests. 13. Changes We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable law or connected services change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
