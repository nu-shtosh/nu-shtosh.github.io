First Days Support Need help or found a bug? Contact us: hi@wordspire.app We will reply as soon as possible. Reporting a bug Please include: - First Days version; - iPhone or iPad model; - iOS version; - steps leading to the problem; - expected result; - actual result; - whether the problem happens repeatedly. You may attach a screenshot, but please remove children’s names, birth dates, medical information and other sensitive data first. Subscriptions Manage your subscription in: iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → First Days If Premium is unavailable after purchase, open the subscription screen in First Days and select “Restore Purchases.” Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Notifications Check: iPhone Settings → Notifications → First Days Also verify that the relevant reminder is enabled and has the correct date and time. Diary data Diary information is stored locally on the device. Export important records before deleting the app. We cannot recover a deleted local diary without an existing backup or export. Privacy Do not send complete diary exports, walking routes, medical information or child data unless they are necessary to resolve your request.
